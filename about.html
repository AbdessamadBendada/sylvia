01 / About the founder

Sylvia Cebanu is a Strategic Finance Partner to Startups and Fractional CFO with 14 years of experience in corporate finance and 7 years operating at C-level across the US, UK, and EU.

Having built financial foundations for more than 100 startups from pre-seed to scale-up, Sylvia's work spans some of the most complex, fast-moving sectors in the startup world: fintech, SaaS, AI, robotics, edtech, biotech, and media.

Founder and investor fluency

She bridges the gap between a great product and a fundable business.

What makes Sylvia rare is the combination she brings: she builds the models herself, sits at board level, and speaks both founder and investor fluently. She knows what each side needs to hear and she knows how to bridge the gap between a great product and a fundable business.

Today, through Digit Finance, Sylvia works as a strategic finance partner to ambitious founders at the most critical stages of their growth from building their first investor-ready financial model, to preparing for Series A scrutiny, to walking into a VC meeting with numbers they can confidently defend.

Every number has a story. Sylvia and her team make sure yours has a happy ending.
