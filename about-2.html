About the founder

Sylvia Cebanu is a Strategic Finance Partner to startups and Fractional CFO with 14 years of experience in corporate finance and 7 years operating at C-level across the US, UK, and EU.

Having built financial foundations for more than 100 startups from pre-seed to scale-up, Sylvia’s work spans some of the most complex, fast-moving sectors in the startup world: fintech, SaaS, AI, robotics, edtech, biotech, and media.
